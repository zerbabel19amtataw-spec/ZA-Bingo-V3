Help & Rules

How to Play

  1. Join a room and pick up to 2 cartelas
  2. Wait for other players to get ready
  3. When the countdown finishes, numbers will be called
  4. Mark numbers or use Auto Mark
  5. First to complete a pattern wins the prize

Winning Patterns

  • Line: Any horizontal row
  • Column: Any vertical column
  • Diagonal: Either corner-to-corner
  • Four Corners: All four corner squares
  • Full House: All 25 squares
